## (3) New defects

**Objection: attribution upgrade in (b).** The added clause says:

> 거기에 있으면 그 역할을 옮기거나 위치를 적는다.

The sentence before it is about transcribing one named credit row, such as a booklet or distributor page. As written, a role found somewhere else (a search snippet, or a fetch of a different page) can be "옮기" into that row. The appendix would then show it as part of the authoritative row. That upgrades the role's attribution, inflates its status, and hides from the auditor which source actually lists the role. That last point is what D6 depends on.

In (a) this is less of a risk, because ③ already has an evidence column.

Replacement text:

> 빠진 역할을 ‘받지 못했다’고 적기 전에 이미 받은 자료와 대조하고, 거기에 있으면 그 역할을 원래 출처와 함께 따로 옮기거나 위치를 적는다. 다른 출처에서 찾은 역할을 옮기고 있는 행에 합치지 않는다.

This adds about 60 bytes. It asks for no new section or list and no counting. It only says where an existing item goes.
