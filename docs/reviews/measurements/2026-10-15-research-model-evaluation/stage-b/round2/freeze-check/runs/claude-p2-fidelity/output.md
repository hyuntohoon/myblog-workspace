**The two problems in point 2:**

> `점검표나 근거 부록에 이름이 있는 크레딧 행(…)을 옮길 때는`

"이름이 있는" means "named in the checklist or appendix", not "copied into it". A producer can read this as covering any row named there, wherever it gets copied, including body prose. The narrow scope that was meant to prevent length growth is therefore not clearly stated.

> `일부만 옮기면 선별 전사라고 밝히고 빠진 역할의 종류를 적는다.`

The overlap requires declaring **what** a selective copy omits. This line only asks for the omitted role types. A producer can drop one co-writer from a role it keeps and still comply. Dropping people within a row was the most frequent D6 defect in round 1, so this wording permits the main failure the point was meant to stop.

**Replacement (whole line):**

> 부클릿·유통사·판매처 페이지 등 출처를 밝힌 크레딧 행을 점검표나 근거 부록에 옮길 때는 대상 트랙에 대해 그 행의 인물과 역할을 모두 옮긴다. 일부만 옮기면 선별 전사라고 밝히고 빠진 역할의 종류를 적는다. 선별은 역할 단위로만 한다. 옮기는 역할은 그 역할을 맡은 인물을 모두, 함께 맡은 것으로 옮긴다.

This keeps the recording duty the same size: it still lists omitted role types only. It closes the gap by defining selection as whole roles only.
