On the drafter’s choices:

- **§5 top-3 timing: ACCEPT.** Pre-task selection protects the mandatory top-3 check better than my exposure-recording compromise. Preserved maps, symmetric amendments and documented removal restrictions retain the necessary safeguards.
- **Unassessable rule: ACCEPT.** §2 adopts my stricter rule. It governs high-risk claims even though §8’s abbreviated checklist mentions only central unresolved claims.
- **§4 class assignment: ACCEPT.** D1–D7 remain acceptance-critical. D8–D10 can be descriptive within this narrow acceptance claim; D11 remains mandatory through the benefit criteria.

The new material defect is §8’s comparator:

> “contain no more adjudicated factual errors than the paired baseline outline;  
> cover at least the same proportion of that album’s frozen top-3 essentials.”

Equal error counts can conceal greater error severity. Equal top-3 coverage can conceal consequential omissions elsewhere in the initial or amended map. Thus, an outline could lose consequential usable evidence and still qualify for a time-saving pass. Candidate-output floors establish minimum adequacy; they do not establish comparative non-regression.

Replace the **Comparable usable evidence** bullet and its two sub-bullets with:

> **Comparable usable evidence** is scored by the post-task audit for each block against the same adequacy standard in both arms. The candidate-assisted outline must contain no more adjudicated factual errors overall, and no more errors at or above each frozen severity level, than the paired baseline outline. It must cover at least the same proportion of its album’s frozen top-3 essentials and achieve at least the baseline’s essential-recall proportion against each album’s initial and amended maps. The audit must also establish no consequential loss of usable evidence under comparison rules frozen before confirmation. If the required comparison cannot be assessed sufficiently, the time-saving verdict is deferred.

No other new material defect found.

Two non-material edits:

- §6.1: rename “Freeze before calibration” to “Freeze before confirmation”; §6.4 separately fixes reliability criteria before calibration.
- §8.1: say “no unresolved central or high-risk claim, and no central unresolved gate allegation” to mirror §2 explicitly.
