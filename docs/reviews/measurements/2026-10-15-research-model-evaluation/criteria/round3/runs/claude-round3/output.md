## 3. New material defects from the merge

**A. "High-risk" is never defined, and unresolved peripheral gate allegations can sit next to a pass.**

- "High-risk" now controls deferral (§2) and mandatory owner verification (§7), but the RC never defines it. Whether an output gets deferred or passes then becomes a case-by-case decision.
- G1, G4–G7 and G9 apply only to central claims, so the only gate allegations that can arise on a peripheral claim are G2, G3a, G3b and G8. These gates bind regardless of where the claim sits. Under my round-2 rule, which the RC copies, an unresolved peripheral fabrication or false-reading allegation is just "listed next to the verdict". That would let "Passes v1" stand beside an unresolved fabrication allegation.
- Because G2 now requires established fabrication, "unresolved" only occurs when adjudication could not be completed. Deferral is the honest result in that case.

Add to §2, after the audit-status table:

> **High-risk claim:** central or not, a claim that (i) is the subject of an alleged gate failure; (ii) is a direct quotation, a creator-statement attribution, a translated quote or consequential paraphrase, or a credit, edition or sample claim; or (iii) the adjudicator judges would be S1 if wrong. The classes are frozen in the scoring manual (§6.1).

In §3, replace the two "Unresolved allegations" bullets with:

> - any unresolved allegation makes the note **deferred**, whatever the claim's centrality;
> - every unresolved allegation is listed next to the verdict with its reason.

**B. §8.1 conflicts with §2.** §8.1 requires "no central unresolved allegation or claim". §2 defers on any unresolved *central or high-risk* claim. Under §8.1 as written, a note with an unresolved high-risk peripheral claim would pass the screen even though §2 defers it. Replace the second sub-bullet of §8.1 with:

> - have no owner-confirmed gate failure, no unresolved gate allegation (§3) and no unresolved central or high-risk claim (§2);
