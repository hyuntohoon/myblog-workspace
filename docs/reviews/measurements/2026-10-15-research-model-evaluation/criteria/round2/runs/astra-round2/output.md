FREEZE AFTER LISTED FIXES

The draft incorporates much of my round-1 position accurately. The remaining objections concern verdict validity and attribution, not the owner’s fixed resource decisions. They require contract changes, not a larger panel.

## 1. Scope — SIGN

The owner-specific scope and deployment manifest appropriately limit generalization. Environment-specific benefit claims need the clarification in §8 below.

## 2. Units — MATERIAL OBJECTION

**Centrality:** The operational definition makes three observable indicators exhaustive. A consequential claim outside those categories could escape mandatory verification and centrality-dependent gates.

Replace the central-claim paragraph with:

> **Central claim:** a claim whose acceptance, rejection or misinterpretation could materially affect the critic’s factual account, listening inquiry, interpretation or research decisions. Priority placement, answering a top-3 essential question and recorded owner reliance are indicators, not an exhaustive definition. Placement outside those categories does not exempt a consequential claim. Owner reliance is recorded before adjudication.

**Unassessable claims:** Permitting up to a quarter of central claims to remain unassessable contradicts mandatory central-claim verification and could allow acceptance despite unresolved consequential errors.

Replace the final two sentences of the audit-status paragraph with:

> Unassessable claims are reported separately, with their number, centrality, risk and reasons. An unresolved central or high-risk claim makes the affected confirmatory output unassessable and acceptance deferred. Remaining claims may be sampled under the frozen audit procedure; their unassessable results and audit coverage remain visible alongside precision.

## 3. Integrity gates — MATERIAL OBJECTION

**G2 conflates unsupported attribution with fabrication.** Preserved content lacking a passage establishes a support problem, not necessarily invention.

Replace G2 with:

> **G2:** Established fabrication of a source or quotation. The adjudication must establish invention or falsification; unsuccessful retrieval, an absent passage or a mismatch with preserved content alone is classified as unsupported, incorrect or unassessable as warranted, rather than automatically as fabrication.

**G3 prohibits truthful audio-access reporting even though §1 permits audio access, and treats retrieval traces too categorically.**

Replace G3 with:

> **G3:** Falsely states or implies that the producer listened to audio, presents a sonic description as its own observation without the claimed audio access, or falsely claims to have read a source. False access must be established by evidence, accounting for permitted access paths and trace completeness; a missing retrieval entry alone is insufficient.

**G9 restricts the unsupported-verification gate to model memory**, whose origin may be impossible to establish.

Replace G9 with:

> **G9:** A central unsupported claim presented as verified, including a claim drawn from model memory. The adjudication establishes the unsupported assertion and its presentation as verified; it need not establish the producer’s internal origin for the claim. An unassessable claim is handled under §2.

Replace the blanket sentence “Without a snapshot it yields *unassessable*” with:

> Where missing preservation prevents adjudication and no other evidence resolves the issue, the result is unassassessable; missing preservation does not override independently established support or error.

Use **“unassessable”** in the final text above; the spelling correction is intentional.

## 4. Dimensions and floor policy — MATERIAL OBJECTION

Reporting an unreliable dimension without a binding floor can be legitimate, but cannot silently remove a necessary acceptance requirement. Reliability also does not establish that a floor distinguishes adequate from inadequate evidence.

Replace floor-policy item 3 with:

> A numerical floor binds only after its scoring reliability and its relationship to consequential adequacy have been established through §6. Before confirmation, classify each dimension as acceptance-critical or descriptive and freeze that classification. If an acceptance-critical dimension cannot be assessed sufficiently, acceptance is deferred until an adequate rule is established; it cannot be made report-only merely because calibration failed. Descriptive dimensions may remain report-only. Integrity gates always bind.

The reception rule is acceptable, provided provenance remains claim-specific: a review is a direct source for its own judgment, while material it relays requires appropriate attribution.

## 5. Reference map — MATERIAL OBJECTION

The draft protects the owner’s task from contamination but does not explicitly protect initial essentiality adjudication from producer-output contamination. Both protections are needed.

Replace the second bullet with:

> After the owner’s task for that album, the owner reviews the initial mapping passes and labels essentiality and the top-3 while candidate and control outputs remain hidden. Preserve this initial adjudicated map before exposing output-derived discoveries. Then apply the frozen amendment procedure. The owner must not previously have seen the reference materials or producer outputs for that album, except the preselected candidate note provided in an assigned candidate-assisted task; record that unavoidable exposure.

This preserves the distinction between an independently assembled initial map and subsequent amendments without pretending that the candidate-assisted owner remains wholly blind.

## 6. Calibration and reliability — MATERIAL OBJECTION

The draft leaves “passes the reliability checks” undefined. It also does not specify whether the first rejected rung itself passes or fails. Either ambiguity could produce arbitrary floors.

Add:

> Before confirmation, freeze the scoring manual, severity definitions, eligible denominators, verification-sufficiency rules, numerical floors, reliability criteria and handling of consequential disagreements. Resolve consequential disagreements against sources and the owner’s task. Owner–model agreement measures annotation alignment, not independent human reliability or correctness. Delayed owner repeats assess decision stability. If the available calibration observations cannot support an acceptance-critical rule, acceptance is deferred under §4.

Replace the sentence about the first rung with:

> Perturbation ladders inform the boundary between acceptable and unacceptable performance. A rung consistently judged to require fixes before use or to merit rejection is on the failing side of that boundary. Natural outputs must also support the proposed boundary; approximately eight examples do not by themselves establish calibration sufficiency.

## 7. Confirmation design — MATERIAL OBJECTION

The task allocation omits randomization, burden omits independent searching and completion work, and the control audit could be used to support an inadequately verified comparative quality claim.

Replace the owner-tasks bullet with:

> **Owner tasks:** Use the same endpoint—an evidence-backed review outline with unresolved questions recorded. Freeze two matched album blocks and randomly allocate one album in each block to candidate-assisted research and the other to the owner’s usual research. Allocate one candidate task to each environment before inspecting outputs. Select the candidate run shown to the owner before inspecting its quality. Record matching variables and residual album differences.

Add:

> Measure all owner active work needed to reach the endpoint, including reading, independent searching, verification, correction and completion. Record collection latency and elapsed availability separately, and include collection work in owner burden when it requires the owner. Report evaluation-only audit costs separately. Audit the resulting evidence and outlines in both task arms against the same adequacy standard.

Replace the final sentence of the audit-allocation bullet with:

> Candidate–control comparisons disclose their unequal audit depth and support only conclusions justified by that depth. Every central and high-risk claim in any output used substantively to establish comparative acceptance must receive owner verification. Otherwise, that comparison remains descriptive and cannot establish non-regression or a quality advantage.

Also add:

> Freeze completion criteria and retry eligibility before confirmation. Any permitted harness retry retains the original failure in completion and cost reporting; it does not erase an assessable candidate failure.

## 8. Pass pattern — MATERIAL OBJECTION

The current wording makes an environment-level pass depend on both matched blocks, although each environment supplies only one candidate task. It also leaves unclear whether both benefits must succeed or whether either separately declared objective can support acceptance.

Replace the opening sentence and item 4 with:

> Report evidence-quality eligibility separately from the time-saving and quality-improvement verdicts. Items 1–3 and 5 are mandatory for eligibility in each tested environment. A version receives acceptance for a declared benefit only if that benefit’s separately frozen criterion also succeeds. Success for one benefit does not establish the other.

> **4. Separate benefit verdicts:**
>
> - **Time-saving:** comparable or better usable evidence with lower total owner active burden than usual research in both matched blocks.
> - **Quality improvement:** a predeclared consequential quality gain within a predeclared additional-time allowance in both matched blocks.
>
> Each benefit receives its own supported, inconclusive or failed verdict under frozen decision rules. Mixed block results are inconclusive for that benefit. Observed differences are reported numerically, with album differences acknowledged; they are preliminary observations for this owner, not a precise causal effect.
>
> Because each environment contributes only one candidate-assisted task, the two-block benefit result concerns the tested configuration collectively. Report environment-specific output eligibility and the observed usability task separately. Do not claim that either environment independently demonstrated a repeatable benefit. A general “passes” statement must name the benefit supported and these scope limitations.

“No evidence of net loss” should not substitute for a positive time-saving requirement: failure to detect harm and observed saving are different findings.
