## 2. Material boundary defect

**D2: §2 contradicts R4.** A-D2-FAIL has only peripheral errors, so it meets the §2 BORDERLINE text ("Errors are confined to non-central claims"). Because §2 overrides the exemplars, §2 silently moves A-D2-FAIL to BORDERLINE, while R4 says it is FAIL. C-D2-BORDERLINE (two peripheral errors) and A-D2-FAIL ("several") are then separated only by an undefined count. That makes the boundary arbitrary.

Replacement text for §2 D2:
> **BORDERLINE:** A few non-central errors; precision on the assessable-claim denominator is reduced but not low (A: one incidental date; C: two peripheral inversions).
> **FAIL:** Low precision on the assessable-claim denominator, whether from many non-central errors (A) or including a central incorrect claim not presented as established (C). The BORDERLINE/FAIL precision cut is set in §6 calibration, not by where the errors sit.

Replacement for the R4 decision cell:
> **Both stand** as FAIL under the amended §2 D2 row; C's is adjacent to G4.

Lesser gap, which I am not counting as a material objection: §2 D4 FAIL does not cover my boundary move to a *meaning-changing* excerpt. Suggested addition to D4 FAIL: "…, or an excerpt changes the passage's meaning (C)."

I found no gate/dimension inversions in §2.

## 3. M1 and M2

**M1: AMEND.** The proposed rule ties the producer's access route to whether the evaluator checked the excerpt against the harness capture. That mixes D5 (how the producer got the evidence) with D2 and D4 (whether the evidence is true and where it is). Replacement:
> A search snippet or model-processed fetch summary is **relayed** for D5, whatever the harness capture later shows. Presenting such a claim as read in primary is an overstatement. Agreement with the harness capture is scored under D2/D4 and does not change the D5 level. Only text the producer received unprocessed from the page counts as read in primary.

**M2: AMEND.** The rule says nothing about the case where the owner cannot decide. Without a fallback, those claims would default to *imprecise*, which is a misleading pass. Replacement: keep the proposed text and append:
> If the owner cannot decide from the evidence, the claim is an unresolved central claim and is deferred under the contract's unassessable rules; it is not defaulted to *imprecise*.
